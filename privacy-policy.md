## English

### Zero Data Collection & Local Operation
QOVX is a native Windows (.NET 10 LTS) system optimization and performance control utility. All memory working-set management, temporary cache cleaning, crash dump purging, DNS flushing, startup item auditing, and visual performance adjustments performed by the application are executed strictly offline and locally on your device.

### No Telemetry or Cloud Transmission
The application does not collect, record, store, analyze, or transmit any personal data, system usage habits, IP addresses, hardware identifiers, or telemetry information to external servers or the cloud. Everything operates entirely offline under your direct control.

### External Links
The application contains zero third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. It only opens external links (such as the developer's official YouTube channel https://www.youtube.com/@ALLAHVERDICAN) when explicitly clicked by the user.

### Contact
If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, you may reach out to us via our official YouTube channel or store publisher profile.
